Frequently Asked Questions

What are managed cybersecurity services?

Ongoing security programmes delivered by an external provider, covering threat monitoring, vulnerability management, awareness training, incident response and compliance, run as a programme rather than a one-off project.

What is the difference between information security and data protection?

Information security protects the confidentiality, integrity and availability of all your information and systems. Data protection is the legal and organisational side of handling personal data: lawful basis, consent, data-subject rights, registration with the ODPC and breach notification under the Data Protection Act. They overlap heavily, and we deliver both.

What does IT forensics involve?

Collecting and preserving digital evidence from computers, phones, servers and networks in a way that keeps it reliable, then analysing it to establish what happened, when and how. You receive a clear report suitable for management, regulators or legal proceedings.

Do we need this if we already have antivirus and a firewall?

They are necessary but not sufficient. Modern attacks routinely bypass both through phishing, stolen credentials and unpatched systems. A programme adds monitoring, hardening, training and a response plan.
